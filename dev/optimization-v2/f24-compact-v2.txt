[Knowledge Card repair-replication-concatenation-syntax@2.0.0-dev.1 | expression-only candidate]
Use only after the development admission predicate confirms the first xvlog 2026.1
VRFC 10-4982 comma diagnostic points to the unsupported replication/concatenation
shape in this candidate and the feedback has not already supplied this method.

Keep a replication as one complete item of an outer concatenation:
{N{expr}} is an independent replication; {{N{expr}}, slice} combines it with a slice.
Preserve the replication count, source bit, slice and destination width; do not delete
the comma/slice or substitute zero extension for required sign extension. Sum item
widths and check boundary/sign-bit behavior after rerunning compilation.

Do not apply to an already valid expression, unrelated or preceding syntax errors,
unsupported macro-expanded syntax, or an unbound/unknown-version diagnostic.
This is a local syntax method, not proof of functional correctness.
